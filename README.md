## 🕹️ About

Honeycomb: The World Beyond is a single-player sci-fi survival sandbox set on the vibrant alien planet Sota7. Play as Hennessy, a bioengineer working for EON Corp, and explore unfamiliar ecosystems while gathering resources, building a laboratory base, and uncovering discoveries that could secure humanity’s future. The Windows PC release is planned for September 8, 2026.

Designed for players who enjoy colorful open-world exploration, crafting, base building, creature discovery, and relaxed sci-fi survival.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🪐 **Alien Exploration** — Explore diverse biomes, open landscapes, and hidden underground caverns across Sota7.
- 🧬 **Bioengineering** — Study alien life and crossbreed plants and animals to unlock new species and discoveries.
- 🏗️ **Base Building** — Construct and upgrade a laboratory sanctuary for resource processing and experiments.
- 🛠️ **Crafting** — Gather resources and craft essential tools to support exploration and survival.
- 💧 **Survival** — Manage food, drinking water, environmental hazards, and unpredictable alien wildlife.
- 🔬 **Scientific Discovery** — Catalog unfamiliar flora and fauna while investigating the mysteries of Sota7.
